💼 Sustainable Deployment & Funding Model

Answering the hackathon challenge: "Who pays: NGOs, CSR funds, banks, local service centres or state governments?"

1. MeeSeva & CSC Kiosk Franchisees (B2B Assisted Civic Tech)
Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs) earn ₹20–₹40 per assisted application under CSC guidelines. SchemeSetu cuts triage time from 25 minutes to 3 minutes, tripling operator daily revenue.
2. Rural Banks & Business Correspondents (BCs)
Banks (SBI, Telangana Grameena Bank) sponsor SchemeSetu kiosks because DBT schemes deposit hundreds of crores directly into savings and PMJDY accounts, earning BCs transaction commissions.
3. CSR Funds & Foundations (Tata Trusts, ITC Mission Sunehra Kal)
Corporate Social Responsibility grants fund frontline deployment to achieve 100% social security saturation across aspirational tribal and rural mandals.
4. State Government (B2G Digital Telangana)
Department of ITE&C integrates SchemeSetu as the official citizen intelligence layer on Telangana's portal, preventing fraudulent claims and reducing physical counter congestion.
